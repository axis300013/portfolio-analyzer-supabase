# Railway Raw Variables Editor - Copy & Paste All at Once

**In Railway Dashboard:**
1. Click your service
2. Go to "Variables" tab
3. Look for **"Raw"** button or link (usually near the top)
4. Click it to open Raw editor
5. **Clear any existing content** (select all + delete)
6. **Paste everything below** into the Raw editor
7. Click **"Save"** or **"Deploy"**
