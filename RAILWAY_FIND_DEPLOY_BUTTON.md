# Finding "Trigger Deploy" in Railway Dashboard

## Quick Answer
**The button is usually in one of these places:**

---

## Method 1: From Deployments Tab (Most Common)

1. **In Railway Dashboard**, click your service card
2. Look for tabs at the top: "Overview" | "Deployments" | "Variables" | "Settings"
3. Click **"Deployments"** tab
4. You should see your latest deployment listed
5. Look for a **"Redeploy"** or **"Trigger Deploy"** button near it (usually on the right side)
6. Click it

---

## Method 2: From Service Card

1. **In Railway Dashboard main view**, find your service
2. Look for 3 dots menu (...) or gear icon on the service card
3. Click it
4. You should see option: **"Trigger Deploy"** or **"Redeploy"**

---

## Method 3: From Settings Tab

1. Click your service
2. Go to **"Settings"** tab (far right)
3. Scroll down to find **"Redeploy"** or **"Trigger Deploy"** button
4. Click it

---

## Method 4: If You Just Edited Variables

After adding environment variables:
1. A **Deploy** button should automatically appear
2. Usually shows at the bottom or top of Variables section
3. Click it to deploy with new variables

---

## What It Looks Like

The button typically says one of:
- ✅ "Trigger Deploy"
- ✅ "Redeploy"
- ✅ "Deploy"
- ✅ "Start Deployment"

It's usually:
- **Blue colored**
- **Located on the right side** of the page or card
- **Appears after you make changes** (like adding variables)

---

## If Still Can't Find It

**Try this:**
1. Go to Railway home: https://railway.app
2. Click on your project
3. Click on your service (the card)
4. Look for the **green checkmark** showing "SUCCESS" or "ACTIVE"
5. Next to it should be action buttons
6. One of them is Trigger Deploy

---

## Alternative: Redeploy via GitHub

If you can't find the button:
1. Go to your GitHub repo: https://github.com/axis300013/portfolio-analyzer-supabase
2. Make a small change (or just push again)
3. Railway will **auto-redeploy** when it detects new commits
4. This way you don't need to click anything!
